# Development branches and research phases

The active development branch is `main`. Create short-lived branches from `main` for fixes/features and merge validated PRs into `main`. No phase-specific Git branching or phase-branch synchronization is required.

The scientific programme is currently in **Phase 1**. Phase 2 is optional and may be skipped. Phases 3 and 4 concern interpretation, analysis and research results rather than separate pipeline development branches.

Existing `phase-N` branches may be retained as historical snapshots but must not control new work. Do not delete or force-update historical branches without explicit authorization.

Preserve the repository's research-method, privacy, `TOMI-LOCKED`, reproducibility, and module boundary rules.
